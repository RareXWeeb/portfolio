Project Dalanet WIP
Project DALANET is a disaster-response communication platform designed to work even when regular networks fail. Using Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, it creates a decentralized mesh network for sharing messages, GPS locations, and status reports without internet or cellular signal. Its goal is to improve rescue operations and community resilience during the critical first 72 hours of a disaster.
